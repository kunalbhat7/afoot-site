Delete your Afoot account

Afoot, by Kunal Bhat

You can delete your Afoot account and its data at any time, in the app or by emailing us.

In the app

  1. Open Afoot and tap your profile picture on the home screen to open Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with Delete my account.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email wayfoldapp@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Afoot account". We'll confirm, and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account, email address and name.
  • Plans you created that nobody else has joined, with all their stops, tickets and uploaded files.
  • Files cached on your phone, when you uninstall Afoot.

What is kept

  • Plans you created that others have joined are handed to another member, so the group doesn't lose them.
  • On shared plans, you're shown as "Removed traveller" and your email is removed. Things you added there, such as stops, suggestions or tickets, stay with that plan for the rest of the group, without your name.
  • Copies in our provider's backups are deleted automatically within 30 days.

More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
