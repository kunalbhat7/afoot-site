Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

Afoot collects only what it needs to let you plan with other people, never sells your data, and lets you delete your account from inside the app at any time.

Afoot is an Android app for planning things together, from a movie night to a two-week trip. It is run by Kunal Bhat ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information Afoot handles, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It applies to the Afoot app (package com.afoot) and the services behind it.

Questions about this policy or your data: wayfoldapp@gmail.com.

We collect the information you give us and the content you and your group add to plans.

What we don't collect: your device's location, your contacts, your photos (other than files you choose to attach), advertising identifiers, or browsing activity. Afoot shows no ads and uses no advertising or analytics trackers. If the app crashes, Google Play may send us anonymous technical crash reports, according to your device settings.

How we use information

We use your information only to run Afoot and keep it working:

  • Sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • Store your plans and show them to the people you plan with, updating everyone's app as things change.
  • Find places, show them on a map and give directions when you search for a location.
  • Turn itinerary text you import into days, stops and backup options.
  • Send sign-in emails and invite links you ask for.
  • Fix problems and improve reliability, using crash reports.

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell it or use it to build profiles of you.

Who we share it with

Your plans are shared with the people on them, and we use a few service providers to run Afoot. We never sell your information.

People on your plans. Everyone who has joined a plan can see its itinerary, members and suggestions. Tickets are visible to the plan's members, and each ticket shows who it is for. Organizers can add, edit and remove content. Anyone you send an invite link to can join that plan.

Legal reasons. We may disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users or others.

Storage and security

Your data is stored with Supabase and protected in transit and by per-plan access rules.

  • All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Database access rules let people see and change only the plans they belong to; files are stored privately and shared through short-lived links.
  • On your phone, your sign-in session is kept in encrypted storage. Tickets you open, or save for offline use, are stored inside Afoot's private app storage on your phone and removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Our service providers may store and process your information outside Canada, including in the United States.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your Afoot account at any time, in the app or by emailing us. We keep your information only while your account exists.

In the app: open Afoot, tap your profile picture on the home screen to open Settings, then tap Delete account → Delete my account.

Without the app: email wayfoldapp@gmail.com from the address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my Afoot account". We'll confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

When your account is deleted:

  1. Your sign-in account, email address and name are deleted.
  2. Plans you created that nobody else has joined are deleted, with all their stops, tickets and files.
  3. Plans you created that others have joined are handed to another member, so the group doesn't lose them.
  4. On plans you belong to, you're shown as "Removed traveller" and your email is removed. Things you added to a shared plan, such as stops, suggestions or tickets, stay with that plan for the rest of the group, without your name.
  5. Files cached on your phone are removed when you uninstall Afoot.

Copies in our provider's backups are deleted automatically within 30 days.

Your choices and rights

You control what you add, and you can ask us for a copy of your data, a correction or its deletion.

  • Edit or remove content you added, directly in the app. Organizers can edit or remove anything on their plans.
  • Leave or be removed from a plan. An organizer can remove you from their plan.
  • Don't use optional features. Google sign-in, place search and plan import are optional: you can sign in by email and type places in yourself.
  • Access, correction, portability, deletion and objection. Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to get a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or object to how we use it. Email wayfoldapp@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Afoot is not meant for children under 13 (under 16 in the EU and UK), and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll let you know in the app before they take effect.
